# Ṛgveda — address concordance

Three address systems for every ṛc, side by side:

| Column | System |
|---|---|
| `mandala_sukta_rik` | maṇḍala · sūkta · ṛc — the addressing this portal uses |
| `ashtaka_adhyaya_varga_rik` | aṣṭaka · adhyāya · varga · ṛc — **Wilson's ordering** |
| `mandala_anuvaka_rik` | maṇḍala · anuvāka · ṛc |

**Why it matters.** The Ṛgveda has two independent divisions of the *same* sequential text.
Wilson's six volumes are ordered by aṣṭaka, which is why his translation could not be attached
to canonical addresses without this table. Anything ordered by aṣṭaka — Wilson, and the varga
labels printed in the margins of the scans — now joins to maṇḍala addressing mechanically.
